Who We Are

Pivotal Accounting Services is a boutique accounting and business advisory firm based in Maynooth, Co. Kildare.


We offer friendly, professional accountancy tailored to SMEs and owner-managed businesses. With over 20 years’ experience working closely with businesses and their owners, we understand the importance of having accurate, up-to-date financial information when you need it.


Our role goes beyond simply preparing accounts. We work alongside you to provide the insight, advice, and support you need to make informed decisions and move your business forward with confidence.

Why Choose Pivotal Accounting Services?

Our mission is simple: to make running your business easier.

Regular Communication

We believe accounting should be about much more than an annual meeting to review financial statements. Whether by phone, email, or in person, we focus on regular contact to keep up to date with your progress.

Business Flexibility

In an ever-changing economy, a rigid approach makes it harder to respond to challenges. We help you put the right structures in place while giving you the flexibility to adapt and pivot.

Responsive Service

When you have a question, you want a clear answer quickly. We pride ourselves on being responsive, approachable, and solutions-focused to keep your business moving forward.

Transparent, Flexible Pricing

We believe professional advice should come with clear and transparent pricing. Our fees are based on the complexity of the work involved and the time required to complete it.


Before any work begins, we agree on the scope and associated fees with you so you know exactly what to expect. If additional work becomes necessary, we discuss it and agree on the cost in advance. No surprises.

Spread Your Fees

We understand that managing an annual accounting bill can be challenging for some businesses. To make things easier, we offer flexible payment options, including the ability to spread your fees into instalments that suit your business.

Principal Profile

Barry Smyth BBS ACA

Founder and Managing Partner


Barry is the founder and Managing Partner of Pivotal Accounting. Having completed a Bachelor of Business Studies, he began working in the Taxation division of a medium-sized accounting firm while completing his Institute of Chartered Accountants exams. He later moved to senior financial roles in the SME market, serving as Financial Controller and Group Finance Director.


During his 20+ years working within the SME market, Barry gained extensive insight into what makes a business a success and the internal and external factors that challenge growth.
